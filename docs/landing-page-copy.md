## How it works

1. **Open** your app from GitHub.
2. **Edit** anything on the page.
3. **Hand off** your changes as one pull request.

---

## Features

**Figma-style editing**
Spacing, colour, type, layout, auto layout. On the real product.

**Instant code**
Each edit is saved to the right file as you make it.

**Your design system**
Values snap to your scale and tokens.

**Every copy at once**
Change a component or a set of look-alike cards together, or just one.

**Ask Tovi**
Describe a change, or mark spots on the page and apply them together.

**Diagnose**
Tovi reviews a page and suggests fixes. You choose what to keep.

**Version history**
Compare before and after. Undo anything.

**Clean pull requests**
Small, readable diffs your developers can review.

## Good to know

- Works with React and Tailwind.
- Runs on your computer. Your code stays with you.
- Use your own Claude, OpenAI or Gemini key.
- Nothing merges without your team's review.
