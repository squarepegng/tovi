## The gap

**The last 10% never makes it to the backlog.**

What ships is close to the design, but not quite: spacing drifts, a grey is slightly off, a heading wraps in the wrong place. Each fix is a screenshot, a ticket and a wait, so most never happen.

Tovi lets you make those fixes yourself, in the product itself, in minutes.

---

## How it works

**1. Open your app**
Connect your GitHub repo. Your product runs inside Tovi with its real screens and content.

**2. Fix it by hand**
Select anything and change spacing, colour, type or layout. Values snap to your design system, and each edit is saved to the right file as you make it: no "apply" step, no waiting.

**3. Ask when it's quicker to say it**
"Tighten the spacing in these cards." Or let Tovi review the page and point out what's off. You decide what to keep, and anything can be undone.

**4. Hand off**
One click sends everything as one pull request. Tovi checks the app still builds before anything is sent.

---

## Why it's fast

**Edits land in your code the moment you make them.**

Drag a padding and the class in the file changes with it. There's no AI turning your edit into code, so it's instant, and it's the same every time. The AI is there when you want it, not in the way of every change.

```diff
- <p className="mt-4 text-lg text-gray-500">
+ <p className="mt-4 text-lg text-muted">
```

---

## Stays on your system

Every value shows its place in your design system (spacing step 4, radius lg, colour muted). Off-scale values are flagged, so a quick fix never quietly adds a new grey.

## Who it's for

**Product designers:** fix what shipped without a ticket.
**Founders and builders:** make the product look considered, with guardrails that keep you on your design system and warn when text is hard to read.
**Developers:** fewer "can you move this 4px?" messages. Changes arrive as small pull requests you can review.

---

## What Tovi doesn't do

- It doesn't build apps from a prompt. It works on a product that already exists.
- It doesn't change logic, data or your backend.
- It doesn't merge anything on its own. Every change goes through your team's review.
- It only works with React and Tailwind for now.

---

## Questions

**Do I need to know how to code?** No. You edit visually; Tovi writes the code.

**Will it touch our main branch?** No. Changes go to a separate branch and a pull request.

**Where does our code go?** Tovi runs on your own computer and your code stays there. When you use the AI, the screen and the relevant code go to the AI provider you chose, with your own key.

**Does it replace Figma?** No. Figma is still where you explore ideas. Tovi is for the product once it's built.
