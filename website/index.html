How it works

From your repository to a pull request.

Connect your repo, fix the product by hand or with Tovi’s AI, and hand it to developers as one pull request. Every screen below is the real Tovi.

1

Connect GitHub. Point Tovi at your repository. It installs the app, starts it and opens it with its real pages and data.

2

Edit by hand, like Figma. Spacing, type, colour, corners and layout, on your real components. Values snap to your design scale.

3

AI design improvements. Ask for a change in plain words. Tovi keeps to your design system, explains what it did, and every edit can be undone.

4

Notes that become fixes. Mark what’s off right on the screen. Each note becomes a task, and Apply works through them all.

5

AI UX audit. Tovi reviews a page, or the whole product, and pins a note wherever hierarchy, spacing, contrast or usability slips.

6

Version history. Every stop is saved as a version. See what changed, restore a version, or undo a single edit.

7

Every code change, visible. Each edit is listed with its exact file and the classes before and after. No hidden rewrites.

8

Push to a pull request. One click sends every change to GitHub as a pull request, with before and after images, for your developers to review.

Questions

Good to know.

Something else on your mind? Write to hello@usetovi.com.

What is Tovi?

A design editor for your real product. You open the app your team built, edit it by hand like in Figma or with AI, and Tovi turns your edits into code and a pull request.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You edit visually. Tovi writes the code, and developers review it before anything is merged.

Which apps does it work with?

React apps styled with Tailwind, built with Vite or Next.js. Other setups aren't supported yet.

Where does my code go?

Tovi runs on your Mac and your code stays there. When you use the AI, the screen and the relevant code go to the AI provider you chose, with your own key.

Do I pay for AI credits?

No. You bring your own key from Claude, OpenAI or Gemini and pay the provider directly.

Will it touch our main branch?

No. Changes go to a new branch and a pull request. Nothing merges without your team.

Does it replace Figma?

No. Figma is still the place to explore new ideas. Tovi is for the product once it's built.
