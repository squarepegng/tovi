From live product to pull request

No mockups to keep in sync and no handoff file. You work on the product itself, and the result is code your team can review.

Connect your repository

Paste a GitHub link or pick a folder on your Mac. Tovi installs the app, starts it and opens it with its real pages, data and components.

Edit the running app

Select anything and change spacing, type, colour, layout, gradients or shadows. Each edit is written to the right file as you make it.

Ask Tovi, or let it review

Describe a change, mark spots on the page, or let Diagnose review the product. You approve what it does, and it checks its own work.

Hand off

One click commits everything to a new branch and opens a pull request. Tovi makes sure the app still builds first.

Speaks your design system

Colours show their token names, and every number shows its step on your scale. Values snap to that scale as you drag, and anything off it is flagged, so a quick fix never quietly adds a new grey. Tovi also warns you when text gets hard to read.

Diagnose reviews a page section by section, or every page of the product, and pins a note wherever hierarchy, spacing, contrast or consistency slips. Keep the notes you agree with, edit the rest, then apply. Simple fixes land instantly, and Tovi checks each one with a screenshot.

Questions

Something else on your mind? Write to hello@usetovi.com.

What is Tovi?

A design editor for your real product. You open the app your team built, edit it by hand like in Figma, and Tovi turns your edits into code and a pull request.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You edit visually. Tovi writes the code, and developers review it before anything is merged.

Which apps does it work with?

React apps styled with Tailwind, built with Vite or Next.js. Other setups aren't supported yet.

Where does my code go?

Tovi runs on your Mac and your code stays there. When you use the AI, the screen and the relevant code go to the AI provider you chose, with your own key.

Do I pay for AI credits?

No. You bring your own key from Claude, OpenAI or Gemini and pay the provider directly.

Will it touch our main branch?

No. Changes go to a new branch and a pull request. Nothing merges without your team.

Does it replace Figma?

No. Figma is still the place to explore new ideas. Tovi is for the product once it's built.
